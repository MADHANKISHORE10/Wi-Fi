3)BSS and ESS

BSS (Basic Service Set)is a single Wi-Fi network with one Access Point (AP) and connected devices

ESS (Extended Service Set) is Multiple APs connected together (same SSID) to cover a larger area

Eg, Home Wi-Fi is BSS and office Wi-Fi is ESS

8\)

Difference between IEEE and WFA

IEEE (Institute of Electrical and Electronics Engineers) that Creates Wi-Fi standards (like 802.11)

WFA (Wi-Fi Alliance) is that Certifies devices to ensure they follow standards and work properly



IEEE = makes rules

WFA = checks devices follow rules



9\)

Backhaul is how your Wi-Fi router gets internet

A backhaul network is the part of a telecommunications system that connects the core network to smaller subnetworks at the edge, enabling efficient data transmission from end-users to the central infrastructure.

Types:



Wired backhaul (Ethernet)

Wireless backhaul (mesh Wi-Fi)



Example (Home Wi-Fi):

Internet comes via fiber -> router -> Router sends Wi-Fi -> devices



Properties:

Speed depends on ISP

Latency low in wired backhaul

Wireless backhaul may reduce speed
